Calibrated on the stored grids, by archetype (overhead share of cable length: urban < 10 %, rural > 50 %, mixed between): size, section length, branching, depth, connection points, overhead share and transformer ratings. Each grid passes a planning check: every transformer at 60 % (power factor 0.95), every section within its rating and every node within 5 % of the busbar. Grids are named SYN-<archetype>-NNNN, and a seed reproduces a batch.
